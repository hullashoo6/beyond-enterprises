What we do

You sell coffee equipment online. So do your competitors. We check public listings for the products you choose and hand you a short report showing the listed prices, stock status and delivery terms on each site, so you can judge the comparison for yourself.

  • Listed prices in GBP for the selected versions.
  • Stock status as listed on each site at the time we check.

  • Links to the listings and the time checked, with uncertain matches flagged.
